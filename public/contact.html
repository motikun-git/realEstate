    FAQ

    Questions buyers often ask

    How accurate is the price explorer?

    It uses an estimated price per square foot for each area and project stage, plus the emirate's registration fee. It is a guide for budgeting — your advisor confirms the exact price of a specific unit before reservation.

    What is the difference between New Launch, Ongoing and Completed?

    New Launch projects are off-plan and usually the lowest price per sq ft. Ongoing projects are under construction. Completed projects are ready to move into and usually cost the most per sq ft.

    Can I book a video walkthrough from abroad?

    Yes. Choose "Buyer", mention the project and your time zone in the message, and we will schedule a live video tour.

    Are there fees on top of the property price?

    Each emirate charges a registration or transfer fee, shown in the estimator. Service charges and mortgage costs depend on the project and lender.
